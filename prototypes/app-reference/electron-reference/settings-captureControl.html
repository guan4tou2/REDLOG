tcpdump → scanner.packet_flow沒在跑
設定步驟

補上連線監控看不到的封包：SYN、UDP 這類沒完成握手的掃描。需要 root。

1. Find your capture interface

ip -o link 2>/dev/null || ifconfig -l

2. Capture (elevated — tcpdump needs root/CAP_NET_RAW; adjust the interface)

sudo "[REPO]/plugins/pcap-capture/hooks/pcap-capture.sh" eth0

3. Or keep RAW packets (rotating .pcap on your disk; only the sha256 goes on the chain)

sudo "[REPO]/plugins/pcap-capture/hooks/pcap-capture.sh" --pcap-out ~/redlog-pcap eth0
mitmproxy transparent → scanner.http沒在跑
設定步驟

把本機對外的 80/443 導進 mitmproxy，擷取不理會 HTTP_PROXY 的工具。需要 iptables／pf。

1. Start transparent capture (elevated — rewrites nat rules for THIS host)

sudo "[REPO]/plugins/transparent-proxy/hooks/transparent-proxy.sh" up 8080

2. Stop and remove the redirect rules (also happens on Ctrl-C)

sudo "[REPO]/plugins/transparent-proxy/hooks/transparent-proxy.sh" down 8080
