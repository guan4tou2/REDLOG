# Quickstart: Capture Packs

1. New project → Settings ▸ Capture: "Recorded in every project" lists the
   shell hook, HTTP(S), redlog-session output and the built-in terminal; the
   three packs are off.
2. Turn on "Host monitors": the clipboard/file/process/connection tuning
   appears, and those sources appear in capture health.
3. Plugins → disable `pack-host-monitors`: the pack shows "disabled in
   Plugins" and its sources leave capture health and stop.
