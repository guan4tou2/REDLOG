# Quickstart: Loot Rule Switches

1. Open Settings ▸ Capture ▸ Loot detection; `jwt` and `generic_api_key` are
   unchecked.
2. In the built-in terminal, `echo` a JWT: no loot row, and the token is masked
   when you open the command row.
3. Check `jwt`, save, `echo` a different JWT: a loot row appears.
